About

I’m a junior at Ohio State with interests in product, information systems, and the way people interact with tools and workflows.

So far, my experience has included internal platforms, public-facing research sites, analytics work, and compliance-heavy processes. I’m drawn to projects where technology, communication, and real-world constraints all overlap.

Most recently, I helped migrate and redesign the NSF HDR platform from Google Sites to GitHub, including shared components, searchable content, and workflow-heavy sections used across institutes.

I like writing things down, making tradeoffs clear, and leaving projects easier to understand than they were when I found them. I care a lot about clarity, consistency, and work that holds up after the first version.

What I’ve been trusted with

  • Handling detail-heavy work where accuracy and follow-through matter
  • Working between technical and non-technical teammates

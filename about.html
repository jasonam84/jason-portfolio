ABOUT ME

ja.JASON AMO-MENSAH

I like figuring out why
something feels harder than it should.

I’m Jason, a Management Information Systems student at The Ohio State University’s Fisher College of Business. I’m interested in how people interact with technology, and how research, data, and careful product decisions can make those interactions better.

At PNC, I explored that question through digital banking behavior and customer feedback. Watching session replays and organizing customer comments taught me to look beyond a metric and ask what the experience felt like to the person using it.

At Imageomics, I’ve approached the same question through building: helping redesign and migrate a research platform, implementing navigation and search, and working with designers and research stakeholders. At ODOT, the focus was on practical reporting improvements in a setting where documentation and accuracy matter.

How I work

I start by listening and understanding the task someone is trying to complete. Then I look at the evidence, write down the tradeoffs, and work toward a clear next step. I care about solutions that a team can understand, maintain, and improve after the first version.

That combination of customer understanding, analysis, and implementation is what draws me to product management and digital strategy. I want to help teams make thoughtful decisions about what to build and why.

Helping teams understand digital performance

Making digital performance reporting understandable and useful for nontechnical stakeholders.

A metric needs context

Digital engagement metrics can show what happened without making the next step clear. The opportunity was to connect website and content performance with practical decisions for teams with competing priorities.

Analysis & stakeholder communication

  • Analyzed digital engagement and website performance.
  • Supported analytics reporting and digital strategy recommendations.
  • Communicated findings to nontechnical stakeholders.
  • Helped connect performance patterns with content goals and team constraints.

From performance patterns to a useful report

Review performance

Examine website and digital engagement patterns in relation to content goals.

Interpret the patterns

Identify useful signals and put changes in context rather than treating every fluctuation as a trend.

Explain the findings

Translate analytics into plain-language takeaways that stakeholders can discuss.

Recommend next steps

Support practical recommendations that account for priorities, effort, and available time.

Tools & methods

Google Analytics · Digital engagement analysis · Analytics reporting · Stakeholder communication

Clearer recommendations for the team

The work supported clearer discussions about digital performance and practical recommendations for content and website strategy. This case focuses on analysis and communication; no numerical engagement or conversion increase is claimed.

Lessons learned

The next decision matters

An analysis is most useful when the reader understands what it means for their next decision. I learned to connect the data to a clear question and explain the limitations alongside the takeaway.

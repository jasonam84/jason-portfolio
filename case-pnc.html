02 / THE OPPORTUNITY

The question behind the work.

Digital banking journeys can look straightforward in a flowchart while still creating friction for customers. The opportunity was to connect observable behavior with what customers said, so product teams could better understand where to focus.

03 / MY RESPONSIBILITIES

Where I contributed.

  • Analyzed more than 400 Glassbox session replays to identify friction in digital banking experiences.
  • Evaluated more than 40 digital customer funnels to understand abandonment and potential improvements.
  • Developed Qualtrics Text iQ topic structures and keyword strategies to classify more than 3,000 customer comments.
  • Translated customer behavior and Voice of Customer insights into recommendations for digital product teams.

04 / APPROACH & PROCESS

From understanding to action.

  1. 01

    Observe behavior

    Review session replays and digital funnels to understand where customers encounter difficulty.

  2. 02

    Organize feedback

    Use topic structures and keyword strategies to make customer comments easier to analyze.

  3. 03

    Connect the evidence

    Bring behavioral observations and feedback themes together to frame improvement opportunities.

  4. 04

    Support prioritization

    Translate the findings into recommendations that product teams can consider alongside their priorities.

05 / TOOLS & METHODS

The working toolkit.

  • Glassbox

  • Session replay analysis
  • Funnel analysis
  • Voice of Customer

06 / RESULTS & IMPACT

What the work contributed.

The work gave digital product teams recommendations grounded in customer behavior and feedback. The figures above describe the scope of my analysis; they do not represent conversion gains or improvements after implementation.

07 / LESSONS LEARNED

What I’m taking forward.

Behavioral data and customer feedback answer different questions. Looking at both helps me distinguish an observed obstacle from an assumption about why it happens.
